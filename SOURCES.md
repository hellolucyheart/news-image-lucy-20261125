# Sources — 25 November 2026

Every story on the poster was checked against reporting first published
or dated 25 November 2026.

1. **CIA and ODNI hit by a cyber breach.** CNN, 25 November 2026, citing
   people familiar with the matter, reports that the US Central
   Intelligence Agency and the Office of the Director of National
   Intelligence appear to have been hit by a cyberattack affecting
   classified information. Reuters' world news summary of 25 November
   2026 carries the same report, calling it the latest in a series of
   cybersecurity incidents hitting US government and business.

2. **Zelenskiy confirms Ukraine hit a Russian ship in the Gulf of
   Finland.** Reuters' world news summary, 25 November 2026: Ukrainian
   President Volodymyr Zelenskiy confirmed Ukraine was responsible for
   Saturday's drone strike on a Russian cargo ship in the Gulf of
   Finland, while ally Norway urged Kyiv to exercise caution in such
   operations.

3. **Houthi drone hits Riyadh's tallest tower, 11 wounded.** Reuters'
   world news summary, 25 November 2026: Saudi civil defence announced
   that eleven people were injured in a Houthi drone attack on a tower
   in Riyadh.

4. **Huawei found guilty of stealing trade secrets.** The US Department
   of Justice, 24 November 2026 (reported 25 November): a jury in New
   York found Huawei guilty on 10 of the 12 criminal counts it faced,
   including bank fraud, conspiracy to steal trade secrets and
   conspiracy to defraud the US government, after a three-month trial.
   Huawei rejected the verdicts, saying they lacked legal or factual
   basis.

5. **WHO evacuates staff in DR Congo as Ebola fight rages.** WHO
   Director-General Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus, 25 November 2026: health
   workers were evacuated from a hospital in Akwe, Ituri province,
   after sheltering there for three days to escape fierce fighting.
   The Ebola outbreak in the country has caused 5,775 deaths among
   11,762 cases.
